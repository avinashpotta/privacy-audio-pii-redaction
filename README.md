PII Detection and Redaction in Audio Streams

An AI-powered, privacy-focused system that detects and automatically redacts Personally Identifiable Information (PII) from audio recordings and live audio streams.

The system combines Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR), Natural Language Processing (NLP), and audio processing techniques to identify sensitive information such as names, phone numbers, email addresses, residential addresses, and other personal identifiers in spoken conversations.

Unlike conventional text-based PII detection systems, this project focuses on accurately mapping detected sensitive information back to its corresponding timestamps in the original audio, enabling precise redaction without affecting surrounding speech.

The system is designed to operate entirely locally, ensuring that audio data and sensitive personal information remain on the user's device without requiring external cloud services.

Key Features
- Speech-to-Text Conversion: Transcribes audio using automatic speech recognition.
- AI-Based PII Detection: Identifies sensitive information using NLP and Named Entity Recognition (NER).
- Contextual PII Recognition:Detects personal information expressed naturally in conversations, including names and addresses.
- Word-Level Timestamp Alignment:Maps detected PII to precise segments of the original audio.
- Selective Audio Redaction:Replaces sensitive speech segments with beeps or silence while preserving non-sensitive audio.
- Local Processing: Processes audio locally to maintain privacy and data security.
- Audio Stream Support: Designed to support both recorded audio and real-time audio processing.

Objective
To develop an intelligent, privacy-preserving audio processing system capable of automatically identifying and removing sensitive personal information from spoken communication while maintaining the clarity and integrity of the remaining audio.

Applications
- Privacy protection in call recordings
- Healthcare and medical audio data anonymization
- Customer support and call center recordings
- Secure meeting and interview recordings
- Compliance with data protection and privacy requirements

Project Focus: Artificial Intelligence , Natural Language Processing , Speech Recognition , Audio Signal Processing , Data Privacy
